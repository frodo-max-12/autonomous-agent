What this is

  • A single process, role-staged rather than multi-agent: sales intake → purchase/sourcing → quote analyst → customer quote.
  • Everything is gated by AUTONOMOUS_ENABLED and AGENT_MODE; testing mode emails nobody and drafts everything to the dashboard.
  • Humans only touch what the confidence gate holds.
